= Notifications

Notifications alert you when you are invited to a Meeting or Call, and when reminders for those activities are due. You can access them from most pages using the bell icon in the top navigation bar. The number of unread notifications is shown as a badge on the bell icon, and a brief pop-up banner appears at the top of the screen when new notifications arrive.

image:Notifications-Open.png[Notifications panel open showing a list of meeting and call notifications]

By default, notifications are checked every minute.

{{% notice note %}}
The refresh frequency and maximum number of notifications shown can be configured by a developer. See link:../../../developer/configuration/notifications/[Notifications Configuration].
{{% /notice %}}

== Snoozing and Dismissing Notifications

Each notification in the panel has two action icons on the right:

* *Snooze* (clock icon) — snoozes the notification and removes it from the panel temporarily. It will reappear after the configured snooze interval.
* *Dismiss* (X icon) — permanently dismisses the notification.

image:Single-Notification.png[Notification panel showing individual notifications with Snooze and Dismiss icons]

To dismiss all visible notifications at once, click *Dismiss All* at the bottom of the panel.

=== Configuring the Snooze Duration

You can set how long notifications are snoozed from the *Advanced* tab of your User Profile.

image:Notifications-User-Pref.png[Set Snooze For Notifications field in the User Profile Advanced tab]

{{% notice note %}}
A system-wide default snooze duration can also be configured by your system administrator.
{{% /notice %}}

== Load More

If you have more notifications than are currently shown, a *Load More* button appears at the bottom of the panel. Click it to load 10 more notifications.
