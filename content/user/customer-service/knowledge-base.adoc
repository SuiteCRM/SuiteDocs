= Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base is a repository of articles containing solutions, FAQs and guidance for handling common support queries. When a new Case is created, SuiteCRM automatically compares the case subject against existing Knowledge Base articles and suggests relevant matches, helping support staff resolve issues more quickly without duplicating effort.

== Key Fields

* *Article Title* — The title of the article. Should be descriptive enough to surface in subject-based searches. Required.
* *Status* — Controls the article's visibility: Draft (work in progress), Pending (awaiting approval), Not Published (hidden from use), Published (visible to users), or Expired (no longer current).
* *Article Text* — The full body of the article, including the solution or guidance. Articles are surfaced as suggestions when their topic or text matches a new Case subject.
* *Resolution* — A concise summary of the solution. This text can be copied directly into the resolution field when closing a related Case.
* *Version* — Tracks the revision number of the article.
* *Author* — The user who wrote the article. Required.
* *Approved By* — The user who approved the article for publication.

== Categories

With a large number of articles, they can be organised into categories using the *Knowledge Base Categories* module. When creating a category, enter a name and description. Once saved, articles are added to a category from the *Knowledge Base Articles* sub-panel on the Category record.

== Relationships

Knowledge Base articles are automatically surfaced as suggestions on new Case records where the subject matches article content. Articles can also be linked manually from a Case record's sub-panel.

For information on working with sub-panels and relationships, see link:../../core-concepts/relationships-subpanels[Relationships & Subpanels].

For standard record operations including searching, editing, duplicating and merging, see link:../../core-concepts/record-management[Record Management].
