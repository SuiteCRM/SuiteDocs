= Campaigns

== Overview

You can use the Campaigns module to send and track marketing, transactional and survey emails to selected users, contacts, targets, leads and accounts.

You can monitor how people respond to your campaign. The tracking tools show you who viewed the email, clicked through or opted out, and any emails that were blocked.

{{% notice tip %}}
Before you send your first email, read link:#_how_campaigns_are_structured[How campaigns are structured] and link:#_who_receives_a_campaign_email[Who receives a campaign email]. They explain how opt-outs, unsubscribes and suppression lists decide who your emails reach.
{{% /notice %}}

== How campaigns are structured

A campaign is the parent record for a marketing initiative, for example a product launch or a monthly newsletter. It holds your audience: the target lists you want to reach and the suppression list you want to exclude.

Inside a campaign, you create one or more campaign emails. Each campaign email is a marketing, transactional or survey email, sent to target lists chosen from the campaign.

Unsubscribes are recorded against the campaign, not against a single email. When someone unsubscribes, it affects every marketing and survey email sent from that campaign, but not emails sent from other campaigns.

== Who receives a campaign email

Before sending, SuiteCRM checks each recipient against two main things:

* *Global opt-out*: the *Opted Out* checkbox next to the email address on a contact, lead, account or target record. It is stored on the email address itself, so ticking it on one record opts out every record that shares the address.
* *The campaign's suppression list*: targets on this list are excluded from this campaign only.

Whether these checks apply depends on the type of campaign email:

[cols="40h,20,20,20", options="header"]
|===
|Recipient |Marketing email |Survey email |Transactional email
|Opted out globally |Not sent |Not sent |Sent
|On this campaign's suppression list (By Id) |Not sent |Not sent |Sent
|On another campaign's suppression list only |Sent |Sent |Sent
|===

Some checks apply to every type of campaign email, including transactional emails. SuiteCRM does not send to a recipient who:

* has no primary email address, or an invalid one
* has an email address marked as invalid
* is on one of the campaign's By Address or By Domain suppression lists
* shares an email address with another recipient, when Duplicate Handling is set to prevent sending to duplicate email addresses

If your administrator has turned on the Opt In Settings, marketing and survey emails are also blocked for addresses that have not opted in or confirmed their opt in.

{{% notice warning %}}
Transactional emails ignore global opt-out and the campaign's unsubscribe (By Id) suppression list. Use them only for messages the recipient needs, such as account or billing notices. Sending promotional content as a transactional email overrides your recipients' choice to stop receiving marketing.
{{% /notice %}}

== What happens when someone unsubscribes

When a recipient clicks the unsubscribe link in a marketing or survey email, SuiteCRM adds their record to the campaign's suppression list. They will not receive any more marketing or survey emails from that campaign.

The unsubscribe link does not opt them out globally. They will still receive marketing emails from other campaigns, unless their email address is opted out.

{{% notice note %}}
If a campaign has no By Id suppression list, for example because you removed it, an unsubscribe opts the recipient out globally instead. When you create a campaign without choosing a By Id suppression list, SuiteCRM creates one for you.
{{% /notice %}}

{{% notice tip %}}
If someone asks to stop receiving all marketing email, tick the *Opted Out* checkbox next to their email address on their record.
{{% /notice %}}

*Example*: Anna unsubscribes from the March newsletter, sent from the Newsletters campaign. She will not receive the April newsletter from the same campaign. She will still receive the invitation sent from the Product Launch campaign, because her unsubscribe only applied to Newsletters.

=== People with more than one record

The suppression list holds records, not email addresses. If the same person exists as more than one record, for example as a lead and a contact, only the record they unsubscribed from is added to the list. Their other records can still receive marketing emails from the campaign.

{{% notice tip %}}
To prevent this, set link:#_duplicate_handling[Duplicate Handling] to *Prevent sending to duplicate records and email addresses* on each campaign email. If someone asks to stop receiving all marketing email, tick *Opted Out* next to their email address instead.
{{% /notice %}}

== Checking why someone did not receive an email

To see who was not sent a campaign email, open the campaign email and go to the *Suppressed/Blocked* subpanel. The *Activity Type* column shows the reason:

[cols="40h,60", options="header"]
|===
|Activity type |Reason
|`blocked-opted-out` |The recipient's email address is opted out globally, or has not opted in or confirmed its opt in.
|`blocked-suppressed-by-id-list` |The recipient's record is on the campaign's suppression list, usually because they unsubscribed from an earlier email in this campaign.
|`blocked-suppressed-by-email-list` |The recipient's email address is on one of the campaign's By Address suppression lists.
|`blocked-suppressed-by-domain-list` |The recipient's email domain is on one of the campaign's By Domain suppression lists.
|`blocked-duplicate-email` |Another record with the same email address is on this email's target lists, so SuiteCRM sent to only one of them. This happens when Duplicate Handling is set to prevent sending to duplicate email addresses.
|`blocked-no-primary-email` |The record has no primary email address.
|`blocked-not-valid-email` |The email address is not in a valid format.
|`blocked-email-marked-invalid` |The email address is marked as invalid.
|`blocked-target-not-found` |SuiteCRM could not find the record, for example because it has been deleted.
|`blocked-validation-exception` |An unexpected error stopped SuiteCRM checking the recipient.
|===

== Create a campaign

From the Campaigns module, select *Create Campaign* from the module dropdown.

image:Campaign-Edit.png[title="Campaigns create view"]

=== Overview

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|*Name*|The name of the campaign. Required.
|*Status*|The status of the campaign: Planning, Active, Inactive or Complete. Required.
|*Start Date*|A date for your reference only. It does not control when the campaign starts sending.
|*End Date*|A date for your reference only. It does not control when the campaign stops sending. Required.
|*Description*|An optional description of the campaign.
|*Target Lists*|The target lists your campaign will send to.
|*Suppression Lists*|Records on a suppression list will not receive marketing or survey emails from this campaign. When a recipient unsubscribes using the link in a marketing or survey email, their record is added to the suppression list (By Id). If you do not add one when creating the campaign, SuiteCRM creates one for you. For more information on suppression lists, see link:../target-lists/#_target_list_types[Target Lists].
|*Assigned To*|The user responsible for the campaign.
|===

{{% notice note %}}
The target lists you select here are the only ones you can choose on the campaign email.
{{% /notice %}}

=== Budget

[cols="30,70"]
|===
|*Budget*|The budget for the campaign.
|*Expected Cost*|The expected cost of the campaign.
|*Actual Cost*|The actual cost of the campaign.
|*Expected Revenue*|The expected revenue of the campaign.
|*Objective*|The objective of the campaign.
|*Impressions*|How many impressions the campaign has had.
|===

== Campaign actions panel

You can now create your campaign email. The *Campaign Actions* panel has four actions. *New Marketing Email* and *New Transactional Email* are buttons. *New Survey Email* and *New Survey* are in the *Actions* dropdown.

image:Campaign-Actions-Table.png[title="Campaign Actions panel"]

[cols="30,70"]
|===
|*New Marketing Email*|Creates a marketing campaign email. It must have an unsubscribe link in the email body. To add one, see link:#_opt_out_tracker[Opt out tracker].
|*New Transactional Email*|Creates a transactional campaign email. It must not have an unsubscribe link in the email body. Transactional emails are sent regardless of global opt-out or the campaign's unsubscribe suppression list. See link:#_who_receives_a_campaign_email[Who receives a campaign email].
|*New Survey Email*|Creates a survey campaign email. Like a marketing email, it must have an unsubscribe link.
|*New Survey*|Creates a new survey.
|===

== Creating a campaign email

image:create-email-marketing-demo.gif[title="Creating a campaign email"]

=== Campaign email fields

*Status*

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|*Draft*|While in draft, the campaign email will not start sending at the *Scheduled Run Date*. Draft is the only status where you can edit the campaign email or send test emails.
|*Scheduled*|When scheduled, the campaign email will start sending in batches at the *Scheduled Run Date*. The batch size is set by the *Number of emails processed/sent per batch* setting in link:#_campaign_settings[Campaign settings]. Select *Schedule* to set this status. To set it back to draft, select *Unschedule*. You cannot do this once sending has started.
|*Paused*|The campaign email pauses if it reaches the *Error threshold per batch* in link:#_campaign_settings[Campaign settings], if the outbound email account cannot connect to SMTP, or if no outbound email account is configured. Queueing and sending both stop until you select *Resume*. While it is paused, a warning banner shows the most recent error from the latest failed email, or the SMTP connection error.
|*Pending Send*|If the campaign email is Scheduled and the *Scheduled Run Date* has passed, it shows as pending send and will be picked up the next time the scheduler runs. You can no longer unschedule it. To stop the process, select *Abort*.
|*Sending*|Emails are being queued or have been queued, and sending has started. You can no longer unschedule it. To stop the process, select *Abort*.
|*Sent*|SuiteCRM has sent, or tried to send, the email to every target.
|*Aborted*|The campaign email was aborted while it was sending.
|===

*Queueing Status*

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|*Not Started*|Nothing from this campaign email has been queued yet.
|*In Progress*|The scheduler has picked up this campaign email and started queuing emails. How many it queues at a time depends on the *Number of emails processed/sent per batch* setting.
|*Finished*|All emails have been queued. Sending may still be in progress.
|===

*Other fields*

[cols="25,75"]
|===
|*Name*|The name of the campaign email. Required.
|*From*|The outbound email account the campaign email is sent from. Required.
|*Target List(s)*|The target lists the campaign email is sent to once it is scheduled. Required.
|*Scheduled Run Date*|The date and time the campaign email becomes ready to send. Required.
|*Marketing Type*|Marketing, transactional or survey. It is set by the action you used to create the campaign email.
|*Tracker Links Enabled*|Turns on trackers for this campaign email. If trackers are turned off globally, you cannot turn them on here. For more about trackers, see link:#_tracker_urls[Tracker URLs].
|*Duplicate Handling*|See the table below.
|*Related Campaign*|The campaign this campaign email belongs to.
|*Subject*|The subject line of the campaign email.
|*Body*|The content of the campaign email.
|===

{{% notice note %}}
Sending does not start at exactly the *Scheduled Run Date*. SuiteCRM picks up the campaign email the next time the campaign email schedulers run, so the actual start depends on how often your schedulers are set to run. You can check this in the link:#_campaign_email_diagnostics[campaign email diagnostics].
{{% /notice %}}

==== Duplicate Handling

[cols="40,60"]
|===
|*Prevent sending to duplicate records*|If a record is on more than one target list, it only gets the email once.
|*Prevent sending to duplicate records and email addresses*|Each email address only gets the email once. If two records share an email address, SuiteCRM sends to one and blocks the other. This also stops an email reaching someone who unsubscribed under a different record with the same email address. This is the default, and we recommend it for marketing and survey emails.
|===

==== Using a template

Select *Insert Template* at the top of the page to choose an existing email template and edit it, or leave the body empty and write the email yourself.

{{% notice tip %}}
To use your own HTML, select the code icon in the body toolbar and paste your HTML into the editor.
{{% /notice %}}

==== Body toolbar

image:Body-Toolbar.png[title="Body toolbar"]

Hover over a button to see what it does, for example *Bold* or *Italic*.

As well as the standard editor buttons, there are:

[cols="30,70", options="header"]
|===
|Button |Description
|*Insert Variable* |The `{*}` icon at the left end of the toolbar. Lets you insert placeholder variables into the email.
|*Unsubscribe Link* |The envelope icon with a cross, near the right end of the toolbar. Adds an unsubscribe link to the email body.
|*Code* |The `</>` icon at the right end of the toolbar. Switches to a code editor so you can edit the HTML.
|===

For more about inserting variables, see link:../../daily-activities/emails/email-compose/#_variable_injection[Composing emails].

== Tracker URLs

You can use tracker URLs to add a link to your organisation's website or to a new product you have launched, for example. SuiteCRM adds a unique ID to the link for each recipient, so it can track who clicks through.

You can see the results as a whole in the record charts, or per recipient in the subpanels below the record.

image:track_recipient_activity.gif[title="Recipient activity"]

To turn on trackers globally, go to *Admin* > *Campaign Email Settings*. See link:#_campaign_settings[Campaign settings].

=== How to add trackers

To add a tracker, add a hyperlink to the *Body* field of your campaign email.

image:Tracker.gif[title="Adding a tracker link"]

=== Opt out tracker

An unsubscribe link lets the recipient stop receiving marketing and survey emails from this campaign. It does not opt them out of other campaigns. See link:#_what_happens_when_someone_unsubscribes[What happens when someone unsubscribes].

To add an opt-out tracker to your campaign email, select *Unsubscribe Link* in the body toolbar. This adds an unsubscribe link to the email.

{{% notice info %}}
An unsubscribe link is required on both marketing and survey campaign emails.
{{% /notice %}}

== Campaign email actions

=== Send test email

You can send a test email to a user, a test target list or a specific email address.

image:send_test_entries_demo.gif[title="Sending a test email"]

This lets you see the email as a recipient would and check it looks right. It also fills in the variables in the email body, so you can check they are working.

Test emails create entries, such as views and clicks, which you will see in the record subpanels and charts.

image:test-entries-demo.gif[title="Test entries in the subpanels and charts"]

{{% notice note %}}
After you send test emails, a warning banner reminds you that the data shown is test data.
{{% /notice %}}

{{% notice note %}}
You can send test emails to up to 50 email addresses at once. To change the limit, change `test_email_limit` in `config.php`.
{{% /notice %}}

{{% notice note %}}
You cannot send a test email once the campaign email is scheduled.
{{% /notice %}}

=== Delete test entries

Removes all entries created by test emails. This action is only available while the campaign email is in draft.

{{% notice note %}}
Test entries are also deleted automatically when you schedule the campaign email.
{{% /notice %}}

=== Schedule

Schedules the campaign email to start queuing and sending at the *Scheduled Run Date*. SuiteCRM asks you to confirm, and warns you that scheduling deletes test entries.

{{% notice info %}}
Once it is scheduled, you cannot edit the campaign email.
{{% /notice %}}

=== Unschedule

Sets a scheduled campaign email back to Draft so you can make changes.

{{% notice note %}}
You can only unschedule before sending starts. After that, you need to abort it to stop the process.
{{% /notice %}}

=== Resume

Restarts queueing and sending if the campaign email has been paused. You see this action when the campaign email paused because it reached the error threshold, the outbound email account could not connect to SMTP, or no outbound email account is configured.

A warning banner shows the error that caused the pause, along with the option to resume.

image:warning-banner.png[title="Campaigns warning banner"]

=== Abort

Stops sending and removes everything for this campaign email from the message queue. You can abort a campaign email that is in Pending Send or Sending.

Just like the parent campaign, you can track recipient activity and send status for each campaign email. The charts are in the Email Marketing Charts panel.

== Campaign email subpanels

The subpanels below the record show each recipient's activity: *Message Queue*, *Sent/Attempted*, *Suppressed/Blocked* and *Bounced Messages*. To see why a recipient was not sent an email, use the *Suppressed/Blocked* subpanel. See link:#_checking_why_someone_did_not_receive_an_email[Checking why someone did not receive an email].

== Campaign settings

You can find the settings for your campaigns in *Admin* > *Campaign Email Settings*.

image:Campaign-Email-Settings.png[title="Campaign Email Settings"]

On this page you can change these settings:

[cols="30,70"]
|===
|*Number of emails marketing records to process per run*|The maximum number of campaign emails processed at one time when the Queue and Send Campaign Emails schedulers run. The default is 3.
|*Number of emails processed/sent per batch*|The maximum number of emails queued and sent per run for each campaign email. The default is 50.
|*Maximum send retries per recipient on failure*|How many times SuiteCRM retries sending to a recipient before marking the email as failed. The default is 3.
|*Error threshold per batch (campaign pauses when reached, 0 to disable)*|The maximum number of errors allowed per batch before the campaign email pauses. Set it to 0 to turn this off, so campaign emails keep sending regardless of errors. The default is 10.
|*Tracker Links Enabled*|Turns trackers on or off globally.
|*Location of campaign tracking files*|Where the campaign tracking files are stored. Choose *Default*, or choose *User Defined* and enter a location.
|*Keep copies of campaign messages*|Turn this on to store a full copy of every message sent to every recipient. When it is off, SuiteCRM stores only the template and the variables needed to recreate each message. It is off by default.
|===
