= App Passwords

Some email providers generate a separate password for use with a specific application, rather than requiring your main account password. This is called an *app password*. App passwords are typically required when you have two-step verification (or similar account security) enabled on your email account and the account you are connecting does not use modern OAuth authentication.

Gmail is used as the example in this guide. Other providers such as Yahoo Mail offer similar app password functionality through their own account security settings.

{{% notice tip %}}
If your organisation uses OAuth to connect SuiteCRM to Gmail or Microsoft 365, you do not need an app password. When setting up your personal email account, set *Auth Type* to *OAuth* instead of *Basic Auth*. See link:../email-setup[Email Account Setup] for details, and contact your system administrator to confirm OAuth is available for your provider.
{{% /notice %}}

== Generating an App Password (Gmail)

=== Enable 2-Step Verification

App passwords require link:https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185839[2-Step Verification] to be enabled on your Google account before you can generate one.

=== Create an App Password

. In your Google Account, go to the *Security* section.
. Go to *App Passwords*. You may be asked to sign in again.
. Click the *Select app* drop-down and select *Other (Custom name)*.
. Enter a name such as `suitecrm`.
. Click *Generate*.
. Copy the generated password from the pop-up that appears. This password will not be shown again — if you lose it, you will need to generate a new one.

== Using the App Password in SuiteCRM

The generated app password is used in place of your regular email account password wherever SuiteCRM asks for one, when configuring a personal email account from your user profile.

To use an app password:

. Follow the steps in link:../email-setup[Email Account Setup] to create a personal outbound or inbound email account.
. When completing the account form, set *Auth Type* to *Basic Auth*.
. Enter the app password in the *Password* field in place of your normal account password.
. Click *Send Test Email* (for an outbound account) or *Test Connection Settings* (for an inbound account) to verify the connection.
. Click *Save* to store the account settings.

{{% notice note %}}
System administrators can configure system-wide outbound email accounts using an app password from the Admin panel. This is separate from the personal account setup described here and is not covered in this guide.
{{% /notice %}}
