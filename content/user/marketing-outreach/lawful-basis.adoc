= Lawful Basis

The Lawful Basis feature allows you to track the legal basis for which a business has permission to store and process a person's information. It is primarily designed for GDPR compliance but is generic enough to use for other purposes.

== Lawful Basis Fields

The feature adds three auditable fields to Contact, Lead, and Target records:

* *Lawful Basis*: the legal ground for processing the person's data (for example: Consent, Contract, Legal Obligation, Vital Interests, Public Task, Legitimate Interests).
* *Lawful Basis Source*: how the lawful basis was established (for example: Website, Email, Phone).
* *Date Reviewed*: the date the lawful basis was last confirmed or updated.

To add these fields to the Edit or Detail view of a module, go to *Admin* → *Studio* → select the module → *Edit View* or *Detail View*, and drag the fields into the layout.

image:LawfulBasisStudio.png[title="Adding Lawful Basis fields in Studio"]

You can track changes to these fields using the *View Change Log* action on a record.

image:LawfulBasisChangeLog.png[title="Lawful Basis Change Log"]

== Updating Lawful Basis Manually

=== Individual Record

Open the record and select *Edit* from the *Actions* menu. Update the Lawful Basis fields and click **SAVE**.

=== Mass Update

To update multiple records at once:

. Go to the List View for Contacts, Leads, or Targets.
. Select the checkboxes for the records you want to update.
. Select *Mass Update* from the *Bulk Action* drop-down.
. Scroll to the bottom of the page to find the Lawful Basis fields.
. Set the required values and click **Update**.

== Updating Lawful Basis Automatically

=== Via Web-To-Lead Form

When *Confirmed Opt In* is configured in *Admin* → *Email Settings*, any Web-To-Person form created in the Campaigns module will automatically update the Lawful Basis fields for new records when the form is submitted:

* *Lawful Basis:* Consent
* *Lawful Basis Source:* Website
* *Date Reviewed:* the date the opt-in link was clicked

=== Via Confirmed Opt In Email

When a contact, lead, or target completes a Confirmed Opt In email confirmation, their Lawful Basis fields are automatically updated:

* *Lawful Basis:* Consent
* *Lawful Basis Source:* Email
* *Date Reviewed:* the date the link was clicked

To send a Confirmed Opt In email manually:

. Go to the List View for Contacts, Leads, or Targets.
. Select the checkboxes for the records you want to update.
. Select *Send Confirm Opt In Email* from the *Bulk Action* drop-down.

See link:../confirmed-opt-in[Confirmed Opt In] for details on configuring this feature.
