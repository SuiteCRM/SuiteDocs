= App Passwords

Some email providers generate a separate password for use with a specific application, rather than requiring your main account password. This is called an *app password*. App passwords are typically required when you have two-step verification (or similar account security) enabled on your email account and the application does not support modern OAuth authentication.

Gmail is used as the example in this guide. Other providers such as Yahoo Mail offer similar app password functionality through their own account security settings.

{{% notice tip %}}
If your organisation uses OAuth to connect SuiteCRM to Gmail or Microsoft 365, you do not need an app password.
See the link:../../../admin/administration-panel/emails/email[Email Settings] documentation for OAuth configuration.
{{% /notice %}}

== Generating an App Password (Gmail)

=== 1. Enable 2-Step Verification

App passwords require link:https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185839[2-Step Verification] to be enabled on your Google account before you can generate one.

=== 2. Create an App Password

. In your Google Account, go to the *Security* section.
+
image:app-password-1.png[title="Open Security section"]

. Go to *App Passwords*. You may be asked to sign in again.
+
image:app-password-2.png[title="Open App Passwords"]

. Click the *Select app* drop-down.
+
image:app-password-3.png[title="Open Select app"]

. Select *Other (Custom name)*.
+
image:app-password-4.png[title="Select Other"]

. Enter a name such as `suitecrm`.
+
image:app-password-5.png[title="Enter suitecrm"]

. Click *Generate*.
+
image:app-password-6.png[title="Click Generate"]

. Copy the generated password from the modal that appears. This password will not be shown again — if you lose it you will need to generate a new one.
+
image:app-password-7.png[title="Generated Password"]

== Using the App Password in SuiteCRM

The generated app password is used in place of your regular email account password wherever SuiteCRM asks for an email password.

=== System Outbound Email (Admin)

. Go to the *Admin* panel.
+
image:suitecrm-system-email-1.png[title="Administration page"]

. Select *Email Settings* from the *Email* section.
+
image:suitecrm-system-email-2.png[title="Email Settings link"]

. Select *Gmail* under *Choose your Email provider*, enter your email address, and paste the app password into the *Gmail Password* field.
+
image:suitecrm-system-email-3.png[title="Email Settings"]

. Click *Send Test Email* to verify the connection.
+
image:suitecrm-system-email-4.png[title="Send Test Email"]

. Click btn:[SAVE].

=== Personal Email Account (User)

The app password can also be used when setting up a personal mail account in your user profile. Enter it as the password when configuring your incoming (IMAP) and outgoing (SMTP) mail account settings. See link:../email-setup[Email Setup] for the full process.
