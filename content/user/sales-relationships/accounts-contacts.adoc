= Accounts & Contacts

== Accounts

The Accounts module is the centralised base from which you can create an
association with most records in SuiteCRM. It is possible to create a
relationship with Contacts, Converted Leads, Opportunities, any Activity
such as Emails or Meetings and Cases. Accounts in SuiteCRM will
typically hold all information specific to a company that your
organisation will have a relationship with. In real world terms an
Account may be a business entity that is a qualified Sales Prospect,
Customer, Supplier or Re-seller and can be used to track all
interactions that take place between these entities and your
organisation.

=== Key Fields

* *Name* — The company name. This is the only required field.
* *Type* — Classifies the account: Analyst, Competitor, Customer, Integrator, Partner, Press, Prospect, Reseller or Other.
* *Industry* — The sector the account operates in, for example Technology, Finance or Healthcare.
* *Member Of* — Links this account to a parent Account. Use this to model corporate hierarchies where a subsidiary belongs to a larger organisation. The parent account's sub-panel will then list all member accounts beneath it.
* *Billing / Shipping Address* — Separate address fields for billing and shipping. Where these differ, both can be stored independently.
* *Annual Revenue / Employees* — Company profiling fields useful for segmentation and reporting.
* *Rating* — A freeform field for recording your assessment of the account (for example Hot, Warm, Cold).


=== Relationships

Accounts act as the central hub in SuiteCRM. From an Account record you can view and manage all associated records via sub-panels:

* *Contacts* — The individuals who work at or represent the account.
* *Opportunities* — Open and historical deals associated with the account.
* *Cases* — Support tickets raised by or on behalf of the account.
* *Activities* — Calls, Meetings, Tasks and Emails logged against the account.
* *Documents* — Files and documents attached to the account.
* *Member Organisations* — Child accounts linked via the *Member Of* field.

{{% notice tip %}}
SuiteCRM includes a built-in Maps feature. A map panel showing the billing or shipping address is available on Account records. See link:../integrations/maps[Maps] for details.
{{% /notice %}}

For information on working with sub-panels and relationships, see link:../../core-concepts/relationships-subpanels[Relationships & Subpanels].

For standard record operations including searching, editing, duplicating, merging and importing, see link:../../core-concepts/record-management[Record Management].

== Contacts

In SuiteCRM a Contact is an individual who is typically associated with
an Account (organisation) or Opportunity (qualified prospect). For
example if Techco is the Account, then John Smith, Sales Manager of
Techco is the Contact. This module holds all information relating to
these individuals and also provides a vantage point for any history
relating to a Contact record, for example if they were involved in a
Meeting, raised a Case or sent an Email.

=== Key Fields

* *Account Name* — Links the Contact to their organisation. Populating this field creates a relationship with the Account record and causes the Contact to appear in the Account's Contacts sub-panel.
* *Title / Department* — The Contact's role and team within their organisation.
* *Reports To* — Links to another Contact record, allowing you to model a reporting hierarchy within an organisation.
* *Lead Source* — Records how the Contact was acquired, for example Web Site, Trade Show, Word of Mouth or Cold Call. Useful for campaign attribution and reporting.
* *Status* — Indicates whether the Contact is Active or not. Can be used to filter contact lists.
* *Do Not Call* — Flag to mark a Contact who should not be contacted by phone.
* *Email Opt Out* — Flag to mark a Contact who has opted out of email communications. SuiteCRM respects this setting when sending campaign emails.
* *Campaign* — Records which campaign this Contact is associated with, if any.
* *Opportunity Role* — When a Contact is linked to an Opportunity, this field records their role in the deal (for example Decision Maker, Evaluator, Influencer). Set from the Opportunity's Contacts sub-panel.


=== Relationships

From a Contact record you can view and manage all associated records via sub-panels:

* *Account* — The organisation the Contact belongs to.
* *Opportunities* — Deals the Contact is involved in.
* *Cases* — Support tickets associated with the Contact.
* *Activities* — Calls, Meetings, Tasks and Emails logged against the Contact.
* *Documents* — Files and documents attached to the Contact.
* *Campaigns* — Campaigns the Contact has been included in.

{{% notice tip %}}
SuiteCRM includes a built-in Maps feature. A map panel showing the Contact's address is available on Contact records. See link:../integrations/maps[Maps] for details.
{{% /notice %}}

For information on working with sub-panels and relationships, see link:../../core-concepts/relationships-subpanels[Relationships & Subpanels].

For standard record operations including searching, editing, duplicating, merging and importing, see link:../../core-concepts/record-management[Record Management].

=== vCard Export

By default, exporting a Contact to vCard format is not available. A system administrator needs to replace the fields *First Name* and *Last Name* with the combined *Name* field using link:../../../admin/administration-panel/studio/#_layouts[Studio]. Once done, a btn:[vCard] button will appear to the right of the *Name* field on the Contact's Detail View.
